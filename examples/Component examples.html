.fab

Floating action button

A persistent primary action that hovers over the content — "New" or "Edit". Not a native Fluent pattern; reach for it when a surface has one obvious action. Extended (icon + label), icon-only, small, and a quiet secondary tone.

In context · pinned bottom-right (.fab--fixed pins to the viewport)

A list or dashboard surface. The action floats over the content on a shadow so it stays reachable as the page scrolls — here it's anchored to this box; in a real page use .fab--fixed to pin it to the viewport.
