Foundations

Built on surface tints and restrained motion

Every surface here stays on the rules: blue is interactive only, red is logo only, surfaces stay white-dominant. Tints and motion are deliberately restrained.

Surface tints

Neutral
#F4F6F8
Pale blue
#EDF3F9
Sky
#E4EEF6
Image base
#E9EDF1

Solid, very-low-saturation grounds for alternating section bands — never gradients, never blue fills behind interactive content. Defined as --surface-tint-* in colors_and_type.css.

Motion — functional, not decorative

Reveal on scroll. Content fades and rises 14 px once, then settles. 550 ms ease-out.
Hover lift. Cards rise 3 px and deepen to shadow-16 at 160 ms.
Arrow nudge. Footer link arrows slide 3 px on hover — a small directional cue.
Reduced motion honored. Under prefers-reduced-motion all reveals show instantly; print and no-JS show full content.
