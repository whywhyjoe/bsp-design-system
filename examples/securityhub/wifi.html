Wifi at a glance

Wifi is usually the first link between your device and the bank — and the link we control least once you leave the building. The practices below keep that link safe in the three places you'll use it: the office, your home, and everywhere else.

At the office

  • Managed BMO devices join BMO-Secure automatically. If a bank laptop ever prompts you for a wifi password, stop and contact the IT service desk.
  • Personal phones and visitor devices belong on BMO-Guest — never on the corporate network.
  • Don't plug personal equipment into wired network ports, and never set up your own hotspot or access point in a BMO building.

Working from home

  • Protect your home wifi with WPA2 or WPA3 and a strong, unique password.
  • Change your router's admin password from the factory default, and keep its firmware up to date.
  • Connect to the VPN before you start work — bank systems and data go over the VPN only.

Public and untrusted networks

  • Treat hotel, café, and airport wifi as hostile: connect to the VPN before anything else.
  • Prefer your phone's personal hotspot over public wifi whenever you can.
  • Never do bank work from public or shared computers — safe wifi can't save a device you don't control.

Something look wrong?

Unexpected wifi prompts, look-alike network names, or a connection that suddenly asks you to sign in again are all worth flagging. When in doubt, report it — five minutes of ours beats a bad day for everyone.

Report it

  • Report lost BMO equipment
  • Submit a Privacy Incident Report
  • Report a suspicious email

    Use the IsIt.Safe button in Outlook.

  • Suspect your computer is compromised

    Contact the Helpdesk immediately, then Report a Security Concern.

  • Report a smishing text message

    Email csoc@bmo.com.
